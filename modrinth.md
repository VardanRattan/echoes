# Echoes — The World Remembers

> *"You can spend 200 hours on a server — build a massive base, die in the Nether a dozen times, be the first person to find the stronghold — and vanilla Minecraft retains zero evidence of any of it. Remove your player, and the world is indifferent."*
>
> **Echoes changes that.**

**Echoes** is an ambient Fabric mod that makes Minecraft worlds remember what happened in them. Significant player moments — tragic deaths, structure discoveries, boss victories, and long journeys — leave behind ethereal, ghostly imprints where they happened.

When you or other players revisit those coordinates, a translucent ghost replay acts out the original moment in place before gently fading into the air.

No intrusive UI. No minimap waypoints. No quest popups. Just a world that feels lived in.

## Key Features

- **Ambient & Immersive**: Echoes trigger naturally when walking near historical locations. Ghosts fade in and out with desaturated skins, gentle particles, and subtle vanilla chime sounds.
- **Authentic Ghost Replays**: Ghosts wear the player’s actual skin (including outer layers and capes), hold the equipment they had at that moment, and perform genuine movement — from crouches to death collapses.
- **Tiers of Significance**:
  - **Whispers**: Faint, fleeting echoes of quiet moments, such as first biomes discovered or early journeys.
  - **Marks**: Clear, glowing ghosts of significant milestones, like player deaths or finding ancient structures.
  - **Scars**: Vivid, dramatic replays marking boss triumphs and marathon expeditions.
  - **World Firsts**: Radiant golden echoes honoring the first player on a world or server to achieve iconic feats.
- **The Echo Crystal**: A craftable survival tool (Ghast Tear + Amethyst).
  - **Search**: Right-click to listen for echoes and release particle trails leading toward nearby memories.
  - **Inspect**: Right-click directly at an active ghost to uncover who they were and what took place.
  - **Record**: Sneak and right-click to leave behind an intentional memory of yourself for others to find.

## Built for Survival & Multiplayer

Echoes is lightweight and designed from the ground up to run quietly in the background without affecting server performance or frame rates:

- **Seamless Performance**: Replay data is compressed and optimized for multiplayer servers.
- **Safe Positioning**: If a player falls into lava or the void, their death echo is anchored to the last solid ground they stood on so it remains discoverable.
- **Player Privacy**: Players can toggle their participation or hide their name at any time using `/echoes optout`.
